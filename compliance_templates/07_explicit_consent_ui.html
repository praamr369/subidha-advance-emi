Explicit Consent UI — Evidence Document

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-CONSENT-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record

1. Purpose

This document records evidence that all customer, partner, and vendor onboarding flows in Subidha Furniture's system use explicit, unticked consent — no consent checkbox is pre-selected, and no bundled or automatic consent is applied to any high-risk flow.

3. UI Evidence — Mockup of Consent Screen

The following represents the consent screen shown during Lucky Plan enrolment (the highest-risk flow). All other flows follow the same unticked-by-default pattern.

Lucky Plan Enrolment — Step 3 of 3: Review & Confirm

Please read each item carefully and tick each box to confirm your agreement. You cannot proceed until all required boxes are ticked.

I have read and agree to the Lucky Plan Scheme Terms & Conditions (Version 1.0, July 2026).
I understand the commercial summary: I will pay ₹ [amount] per month for [X] months. I will receive furniture when my name is drawn. I may exit before my draw and receive a refund less the processing fee.
I consent to Subidha Furniture storing my contact and transaction details for the purpose of this scheme. I have read the Privacy Policy.
(Optional) I agree to receive promotional communications from Subidha Furniture.

[CONFIRM & ENROL] button is disabled until all required checkboxes (first three) are ticked.

Note: All checkboxes above are shown unticked. The "CONFIRM & ENROL" button is disabled by default and activates only after the three required boxes are ticked. The optional marketing consent is independent and does not block the flow.

4. Technical Controls

  • All consent checkboxes are rendered with defaultChecked={false} — no server-side or client-side pre-selection.
  • The submit/confirm button is disabled until all required consents are ticked (enforced in frontend and validated in backend).
  • Marketing opt-in is a separate, independent field and its status is stored separately from legal consents.
  • Consent records are timestamped and stored in the audit log at the moment of submission.
Compliance statement: No customer, partner, or vendor consent is obtained by default, by bundling, or by pre-selection. Every consent record in the system represents a deliberate, affirmative action by the consenting party.
