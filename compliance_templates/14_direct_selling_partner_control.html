Direct-Selling Partner Control

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-DSP-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record

1. Purpose

This document establishes the policy for sales partner arrangements at Subidha Furniture. The policy ensures that partner compensation is purely transaction-linked and that no pyramid, multi-level, or recruitment-linked payout model exists, in compliance with the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021.

2. Permitted Partner Model

Subidha Furniture operates a single-tier referral commission model:

  • A sales partner refers a customer to Subidha Furniture.
  • The customer purchases a product or enrols in the Lucky Plan.
  • The sales partner earns a one-time commission based on the verified sale value.
  • Commission is paid only after the sale is confirmed and the first payment is received.
  • No commission is paid for recruiting other sales partners.
  • No commission is paid based on the earnings or sales of any other partner.

4. Prohibited Structures

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate partner termination:
  • Any payment or incentive for recruiting or sponsoring another sales partner
  • Multi-level or hierarchical commission structures (upline/downline)
  • Commission based on the earnings of another partner
  • Requiring a partner to purchase inventory or pay a joining fee to participate
  • Promises of guaranteed income or passive income
  • Misrepresenting the Lucky Plan scheme as an "investment" or "guaranteed return" product

5. Partner Agreement Requirements

Every sales partner must sign a written Sales Partner Agreement before the first referral. The agreement must contain:

  • The commission rate and basis (transaction-linked only)
  • The payment timeline and method
  • An explicit statement that no pyramid/MLM structure exists and no recruitment commission is payable
  • A clause requiring the partner to accurately represent Subidha Furniture's products and not make false income claims to prospective customers
  • Termination provisions for breach of the prohibited structures above

6. Monitoring

The proprietor reviews partner commission records monthly. Any anomaly suggesting recruitment-linked payments or multi-level structures triggers an immediate review. The system enforces that commission payments are linked to a specific confirmed customer transaction ID.
