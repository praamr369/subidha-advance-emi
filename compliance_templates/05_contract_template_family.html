3. Lucky Plan Membership Agreement — Key Clauses

  • Scheme description: Customer pays a fixed monthly instalment. On the Lucky Plan draw date, one enrolled member per draw cycle wins the right to receive their chosen furniture item immediately without further payment. Remaining members continue paying instalments until their draw.
  • Draw mechanism: Cryptographic random draw conducted by the system on the scheduled draw date. Draw is auditable and tamper-evident.
  • Seller of record: Subidha Furniture is the sole seller, lessor, and collection agent. No third-party financing arrangement exists.
  • Refund clause: A member who exits before their draw date is entitled to a refund of paid instalments less the applicable processing fee stated in the schedule.
  • Governing law: West Bengal, India. Jurisdiction: courts of [District].
  • Consent: Customer must sign or provide written consent before activation. No pre-ticked or automatic enrolment.

4. EMI Sale Agreement — Key Clauses

  • Item: Specific furniture item(s) as per the attached schedule.
  • Total price, down payment, EMI amount, tenure, and due dates: As per the signed schedule attached to each agreement.
  • Late payment charge: As per the current approved Business Rule Policy — label and rate disclosed in writing at signing.
  • Ownership transfer: Title passes to the customer upon receipt of the final instalment.
  • Default clause: Three consecutive missed EMIs constitute default. Seller may recall the item and retain paid instalments subject to applicable law.
  • Governing law: West Bengal, India.

5. Version Control Policy

All templates are version-controlled. Any amendment requires proprietor sign-off and a new version number. Old versions are archived and may not be used for new transactions after the effective date of the replacement version.

Internal note: Physical signed copies of all customer agreements are retained in the shop register. Digital copies (scanned PDF) are stored in the business archive folder. No raw biometric data is collected or stored.
