Signature Tiering Policy

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-SIG-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record

1. Purpose

This policy defines when each type of signature or consent mechanism is used for contracts and agreements entered into by Subidha Furniture and its customers, partners, and vendors. The goal is to match the legal assurance level of the signature to the financial and legal risk of the document.

2. Signature Tiers

TierMethodEvidence RetainedLegal Basis
Tier A — Physical Signature Wet ink signature on printed agreement Original paper copy in shop register; scanned PDF in digital archive Indian Contract Act 1872; highest enforceability
Tier B — Clickwrap / Digital Acknowledgement Customer ticks checkboxes and taps Confirm in the app or web portal Timestamp, IP address, device fingerprint, user ID stored in audit log IT Act 2000, Section 10A — electronic contracts valid
Tier C — Verbal / WhatsApp Confirmation Verbal agreement confirmed by WhatsApp message for low-value items only WhatsApp message screenshot saved in customer file Used only for cash sales under ₹5,000; not used for EMI or Lucky Plan

3. Tiering Matrix — Which Documents Require Which Tier

Marketing opt-inTier B — ClickwrapPreference only; reversible
Cash sale under ₹5,000Tier C — Verbal / WhatsAppLow value; one-time; receipt issued
Vendor supply agreementTier A — Physical signatureBusiness counterparty; supply chain commitment
Sales partner agreementTier A — Physical signatureCommission arrangement; ongoing obligation

4. Current Position on Digital eSign (DSC / Aadhaar eSign)

Subidha Furniture currently does not use CCA-accredited digital signatures (DSC) or Aadhaar-based eSign. All high-tier contracts use physical wet-ink signatures. This policy will be updated if and when digital eSign is introduced.

5. Prohibited Methods

  • Pre-signed blank agreements
  • Signature by proxy without written authorisation from the customer
  • Digital eSign without the customer's explicit Aadhaar/OTP consent (when digital eSign is introduced)
  • WhatsApp or verbal-only consent for EMI, Lucky Plan, or any credit-linked agreement
