1. Purpose

This document records how Subidha Furniture ensures that customers receive a copy of every signed agreement and how those agreements are archived for future retrieval — both physically at the shop and digitally through the customer portal.

3. Customer Portal Archive — My Documents & Archive

The customer portal provides a unified "My Documents & Archive" page at /customer/documents with three tabs:

  • Agreements tab: All subscription and EMI contracts in PDF format. Each entry shows the document type, subscription reference number, version, and date. One-click download.
  • Receipts tab: All payment receipts with receipt number, date, and amount. One-click PDF download.
  • Draw Certificates tab: Lucky Draw winner certificates for draws where the customer won. One-click PDF download of an A4 certificate including batch, draw month, lucky number, EMIs waived, and cryptographic verification hash.

All documents in the portal are scoped to the logged-in customer's account only. No customer can access another customer's documents.

4. Physical Archive

  • Original paper copies: Filed in the shop agreement register by customer name and date. Stored in a locked filing cabinet at the shop premises.
  • Scanned backup: Scanned PDF uploaded to Google Drive on the day of signing.
  • Retrieval on request: Any customer may request a copy of any document. Delivered by WhatsApp PDF or printed copy within 2 working days.
  • Retention period: 7 years from the date of the document or 3 years after the last transaction, whichever is later.

5. Immutability and Security

  • Documents in the customer portal are read-only — customers can download but not modify them.
  • Document records in the system are append-only; no staff member can delete a signed document record.
  • Physical originals are stored off-system; no software operation can alter paper records.
Delivery guarantee: Every customer receives their signed agreement physically at the time of signing, and digitally via the customer portal. No customer needs to request a copy — it is always available to them on demand through the portal.
