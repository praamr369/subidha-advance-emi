Stamp Duty / e-Stamp Control

1. Purpose

This document establishes Subidha Furniture's policy on stamp duty for contracts and agreements executed in West Bengal. An unstamped or insufficiently stamped instrument cannot be admitted as evidence in a court of law. This control ensures that every agreement that requires stamping is stamped before reliance.

Key principle: Electronic signing does not cure stamp-duty defects. A digitally signed but unstamped agreement that requires stamping is inadmissible as evidence until impounded and stamped. Physical stamping must occur before or at the time of execution for covered documents.

3. Stamping Procedure

  1. Before execution of any Tier A agreement (EMI, Lucky Plan, vendor, partner), the staff member prepares the agreement text.
  2. The proprietor or authorised staff member purchases the appropriate denomination of non-judicial stamp paper from a licensed stamp vendor in [District], West Bengal.
  3. The agreement text is written/typed onto the stamp paper or an additional stamp paper is attached and signed across the join.
  4. Both parties sign the stamped agreement. The date of execution must be on or after the stamp paper issue date.
  5. The original stamped agreement is filed in the shop register. A scan is uploaded to the digital archive.

4. e-Stamp (Future)

When West Bengal's e-stamping facility is available for the relevant agreement types, Subidha Furniture will transition to e-stamps via the Stock Holding Corporation of India Limited (SHCIL) authorised collection centres. This document will be updated at that time.

5. Review

This matrix is reviewed annually by the CA and updated for any changes in West Bengal stamp duty rates or the Indian Stamp Act notification status.
