Signature Evidence Package

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-EVP-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record

1. Purpose

This document describes how Subidha Furniture retains, archives, and makes auditable the evidence of contract formation — covering physical signed agreements, digital clickwrap records, and all associated metadata. This design ensures that every agreement can be proven in a dispute.

2. Physical Signature Evidence (Tier A)

  • Original paper copy: Retained in the shop agreement register, filed by customer name and agreement date. Stored in a locked filing cabinet at the shop premises.
  • Scanned PDF: Original scanned using a mobile scanner app at the time of signing. PDF saved to the business Google Drive folder: Subidha Furniture / Agreements / [Year] / [Customer Name]_[Agreement Type]_[Date].pdf
  • Customer copy: A signed photocopy or printed duplicate is given to the customer at the time of signing.
  • Retention period: 7 years from the date of the agreement or 3 years after the last transaction under the agreement, whichever is later.

3. Digital Clickwrap Evidence (Tier B)

For all online consent flows (app onboarding, terms acceptance), the following metadata is captured and stored in the system audit log at the moment of submission:

Evidence FieldWhat Is Captured
User IDInternal customer/partner/vendor ID
TimestampUTC timestamp of consent submission (ISO 8601)
IP AddressClient IP at time of submission
User AgentBrowser / app version string
Consent itemsList of each checkbox label that was ticked
Document versionVersion and effective date of T&C / scheme document presented
OTP statusWhether OTP was verified before consent (for KYC flows)
AcknowledgementSystem-generated acknowledgement ID stored in audit log

This audit log is append-only and cannot be modified by staff or customers after the fact.

4. Evidence Export and Retrieval

  • Audit log records for any customer or agreement can be exported by the admin from the business management system.
  • Physical scan copies are retrievable from the Google Drive archive within 24 hours on request.
  • In a dispute, both the scanned original and the audit log record are presented together as the evidence package.

5. Immutability Controls

  • Audit log entries are never deleted or edited after creation.
  • Google Drive archive is shared read-only with the CA/auditor; the proprietor retains write access.
  • Physical register is stored off-system; no software operation can alter paper records.
Note: If a customer disputes an agreement, the combination of (a) the scanned original with their wet-ink signature and (b) the system record showing the agreement terms at the time of signing is the primary evidence. Staff are instructed never to destroy or alter any agreement document.
