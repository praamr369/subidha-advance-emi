1. Current GST Registration Status

Subidha Furniture is currently GST-unregistered.
The business turnover is below the GST registration threshold applicable to West Bengal for goods supply. All bills issued are non-GST bills. No GST, CGST, SGST, IGST, or Cess is charged on any customer invoice. No Input Tax Credit (ITC) is claimed.

3. ECO (E-Commerce Operator) Posture

Subidha Furniture does not operate as an E-Commerce Operator (ECO) as defined under Section 52 of the CGST Act. The business does not facilitate supply of goods or services by other sellers through a digital platform. Subidha Furniture is the direct seller in every transaction.

Accordingly, TCS (Tax Collected at Source) under Section 52 CGST Act does not apply to Subidha Furniture as an ECO. This position will be reviewed if a marketplace model is introduced in the future.

4. GST Registration Trigger

The proprietor monitors annual turnover against the applicable registration threshold (currently ₹40 lakhs for goods suppliers in West Bengal as of FY 2025-26). When cumulative turnover in any financial year approaches ₹35 lakhs, the CA will be engaged to begin GST registration. At that point:

  • All invoice templates will be updated to include GSTIN, HSN codes, and applicable tax lines.
  • The GST Certificate will be uploaded to the compliance system.
  • This document will be revised to reflect the registered workflow.

5. Advance Receipt Treatment

Advance amounts collected under Lucky Plan or EMI agreements are treated as advance receipts against a future supply. Since the business is GST-unregistered, no GST is payable on advances. Receipts are issued at the time of collection and delivery is recorded separately when goods are handed over.

Important: Staff are instructed never to add GST/tax fields, GSTIN, or tax rate text to any customer bill until the business is formally GST-registered and the CA has confirmed the effective date of registration. Issuing a fake GST invoice is a criminal offence.
