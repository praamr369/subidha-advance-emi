Compliance Templates — Subidha Furniture

All operational SOP documents for business compliance setup  |  July 2026  |  PRIVATE — Internal use only
How to use these templates:
  1. Open each template link below in your browser.
  2. Fill in the [bracketed placeholders] with real details (your name, address, phone, etc.).
  3. Print to PDF: press Ctrl+P → Save as PDF.
  4. Upload the PDF to the Business Compliance page in the admin panel, against the matching template row.
  5. Click Submit for Review → then Approve on each uploaded document.
