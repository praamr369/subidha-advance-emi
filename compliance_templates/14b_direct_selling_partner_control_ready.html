Subscription Referral Commission Policy

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-REF-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record
Current Status: ACTIVE — Referral commission available to existing customers This is not a direct-selling scheme, franchise arrangement, or multi-level marketing programme. It is a simple, transaction-linked subscription referral commission available to Subidha Furniture customers who recommend others to subscribe to the Advance EMI or Lucky Plan schemes.

1. What This Is

Subscription Referral Commission: An existing customer of Subidha Furniture refers a new person to subscribe to the Advance EMI or Lucky Plan scheme. If the referred person subscribes and their first instalment is confirmed, the referring customer earns a one-time, transaction-linked commission. That is the entire arrangement.

4. Commission Terms

  • Rate: Commission rate is agreed individually in writing between the referrer and Subidha Furniture at the time of each referral. Default rate is disclosed in the app/portal referral section.
  • Payment timing: Commission is paid to the referrer within 30 days of the referred subscriber's first instalment being confirmed and cleared.
  • Payment method: By bank transfer or adjustment against the referrer's own subscription balance.
  • TDS: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) will be applied if the referrer's total commission in a financial year exceeds the applicable TDS threshold under the Income Tax Act 1961. PAN is required for commission payments above the threshold.
  • Cancellation: If the referred subscriber cancels their subscription before the first instalment is confirmed, no commission is payable.

5. No Guarantees or Representations

  • Referrers must not make income guarantees or earnings representations to prospective subscribers.
  • Referrers must not misrepresent the Lucky Plan scheme as an investment, savings scheme, or guaranteed-return product.
  • Any referrer found making false representations will have their referral commission forfeited and may be subject to recovery of previously paid commissions.

6. Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021 — Applicability

This referral commission arrangement is not a "direct selling" business as defined under the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021 because:

  • Referrers are existing customers, not appointed sales agents or distributors.
  • There is no network of referrers with hierarchical relationships.
  • No referrer earns income from the activities of other referrers.
  • No referrer purchases products for resale.

Accordingly, the Direct Selling Rules do not apply to this arrangement. However, the spirit of those rules — no pyramid structure, no recruitment earnings, transaction-linked compensation only — is fully adopted by this policy as a matter of good practice.
