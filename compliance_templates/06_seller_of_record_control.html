Seller / Lessor of Record Control

Business: Subidha Furniture  |  Document ref: SF-LEGAL-SOR-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  Date: July 2026  |  Classification: PRIVATE — Internal compliance record

1. Declaration

Subidha Furniture is the sole seller, lessor, and financier on all orders, contracts, and transactions processed through this business. No third-party marketplace, platform intermediary, or co-seller arrangement exists.

This document establishes the operational control ensuring that every sale, instalment agreement, Lucky Plan enrolment, and vendor transaction has one immutable seller-of-record identity: Subidha Furniture, operated by its proprietor.

Sales partners act as referral agents only. They do not issue invoices, collect payments, or hold any seller-of-record status on any transaction.

3. GST Invoice Responsibility

Subidha Furniture is currently GST-unregistered. All bills and invoices are non-GST bills issued under the proprietor's name and address. No GST credit notes, debit notes, or Input Tax Credit (ITC) wording appear on any customer-facing document.

When and if GST registration is obtained, this document will be updated and the invoice templates revised accordingly. A GST Certificate will be uploaded to the compliance system at that time.

4. Operational Controls

  • The software system enforces a single seller-of-record field on every sale, EMI, and Lucky Plan record. This field is set to Subidha Furniture at creation and cannot be changed by staff without proprietor override.
  • All payment collection flows route to the single registered business bank account.
  • No order may be marked "fulfilled" or "delivered" without the seller-of-record being Subidha Furniture.
  • Partner commissions are calculated after the sale is recorded and are never linked to invoice issuance.

5. Review Cadence

This document is reviewed annually or when a new transaction type is introduced. Any change to the seller-of-record model requires proprietor approval and a new document version.
