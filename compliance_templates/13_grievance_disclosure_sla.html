2. Complaint Process

  1. Step 1 — Lodge complaint: Customer contacts the Grievance Officer by phone, email, or in person at the shop. A complaint reference number is issued within 24 hours of receipt.
  2. Step 2 — Acknowledgement: Written acknowledgement (by SMS, email, or letter) within 48 hours confirming the complaint reference number and expected resolution date.
  3. Step 3 — Investigation: The Grievance Officer reviews the complaint, consults records, and contacts the customer for any additional information.
  4. Step 4 — Resolution: A final resolution is communicated to the customer within 30 days of the complaint date. If resolution requires more time, the customer is informed with a revised date and reason.
  5. Step 5 — Escalation: If the customer is not satisfied with the resolution, they may escalate to the consumer forum or relevant authority under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

4. Types of Complaints Handled

  • EMI or Lucky Plan payment disputes
  • Furniture delivery issues — delay, damage, wrong item
  • Refund requests
  • Agreement or contract disputes
  • Privacy / data handling complaints
  • Staff conduct complaints
  • Any other customer complaint relating to a transaction with Subidha Furniture

5. Record Keeping

All complaints are logged in the complaints register (physical or digital) with date received, complaint summary, actions taken, and resolution date. Records are retained for 3 years.
