# Google Business Profile Checklist — Subidha Furniture (owner tasks)

These are manual tasks for the owner in Google Business Profile
(business.google.com). They cannot be automated from this codebase, but they are
the single biggest factor in local "furniture shop near me" visibility.

Keep every detail identical to what appears on the website and on
`GET /api/v1/public/business-profile/`.

1. **Verify** the Google Business Profile (postcard, phone, or video verification).
2. **Business name:** `Subidha Furniture` — exactly. Do **not** add keywords like
   "Best Furniture Asansol EMI" to the name (keyword-stuffing violates Google policy).
3. **Primary category:** `Furniture store`.
4. **Secondary categories:** add only truthful ones actually offered (e.g.
   `Bed shop`, `Sofa store`, `Mattress store`) — skip any you don't really do.
5. **Address:** the exact showroom address in Asansol, West Bengal. Match the
   website contact page character-for-character.
6. **Phone:** the exact business phone used on the website.
7. **Website:** `https://subidhafurnitureasansol.com`.
8. **Opening hours:** real hours; keep them updated for holidays.
9. **Showroom photos:** real interior/exterior photos.
10. **Product photos:** real photos of beds, sofas, wardrobes, dining sets, mattresses.
11. **Products / services list:** add real categories you sell.
12. **Messaging / WhatsApp:** enable if you actively respond.
13. **Reviews:** ask real, satisfied customers to leave honest reviews.
14. **Reply to reviews:** respond politely to all, positive and negative.
15. **NAP consistency:** Name, Address, Phone identical across GBP, website,
    Facebook, Instagram, Justdial, IndiaMART, and any directory.
16. **Link social profiles:** add Facebook/Instagram/YouTube/Justdial/IndiaMART
    URLs only for accounts you actually maintain.
17. **Never buy fake reviews** — it risks suspension and is against policy.
18. **Never keyword-stuff** the business name or categories.

Consistency note: the website's `FurnitureStore` JSON-LD and contact page read
from the admin Public Site profile. Fill that profile with the same address/phone
/hours you enter in Google so structured data and GBP agree.
