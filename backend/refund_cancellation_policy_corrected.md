# REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY (Version 2.0 - CORRECTED)

**Effective Date:** 01-Jul-2026  
**Compliance:** Consumer Protection Act 2019 (CPA), ITA 1961  
**Business Model:** Product Subscription with Monthly Installment Payments  

---

## PRODUCT SUBSCRIPTION MODEL (NOT Gambling/Lottery)

Subidha Furniture's **Advance EMI** is a **product subscription** where:
- ✓ Customer purchases furniture/appliances (product ownership)
- ✓ Pays via monthly installments (EMI) over 6-24 months
- ✓ Optional Lucky Draw feature (waives one month's EMI if won)
- ✓ Product owned by customer regardless of draw outcome

**It is NOT:**
- Lottery scheme (no gambling)
- Chit fund (no pooling)
- Prize scheme requiring license
- Gambling activity per IPC Section 294

---

## 1. CPA 2019 - MANDATORY 7-DAY RETURN PERIOD

Per Consumer Protection Act 2019, customers have **unconditional 7-day window** to return products:
- Counted from delivery date (not purchase/subscription date)
- No questions asked
- Full refund credited within 7 days
- Only condition: Product in unopened/unused condition

**Exception:** Customized/made-to-order furniture (agreed in writing at purchase)

---

## 2. REFUND CALCULATION

### Days 1-7 (CPA 2019 Protected Period):
- **Full Refund:** 100% of product price
- **EMI paid:** Refunded in full (all monthly payments returned)
- **Delivery charge:** Refunded if paid separately
- **GST:** Refunded per ITA 1961 rules
- **Lucky Draw waiver:** If EMI waived before return, credit returned to customer

### Days 8+ (Post-CPA Period):
- **Restocking Fee:** 10% of product price (for store logistics)
- **EMI paid:** Refunded 90% (10% logistics charge deducted)
- **Damage Assessment:** Actual cost if item shows damage
- **Delivery Charge:** Non-refundable
- **Installation:** Non-refundable if completed

---

## 3. ADVANCE EMI CANCELLATION (Subscription Cancellation)

### What is Cancellation?
Cancellation = Customer no longer wants the product + monthly EMI payments STOP

**Note:** Only possible during CPA 2019 protected period (days 1-7 after delivery).

### Days 1-7 (CPA Protected):
- **Full refund:** Product price + all EMI paid
- **Zero penalties**
- **Delivery charges:** Refunded if paid separately
- **Lucky waiver:** If applicable, refunded to customer

### Days 8-30:
- **Refund:** Product price - 10% restocking fee
- **EMI refunded:** 90% (10% logistics deduction)
- **Non-refundable:** Delivery & installation charges

### Days 31+ (Customer-Initiated Cancellation):
- **Refund:** Product price - 10% cancellation charge
- **EMI refunded:** 85% (15% early termination fee)
- **Non-refundable:** Delivery & installation charges
- **Note:** After day 30, cancellation is at company discretion

---

## 4. LUCKY DRAW & REFUND

### If Customer Wins EMI Waiver (Before Return):
- **Waived EMI amount:** Refunded to customer as adjustment
- **Example:** Customer's 5th EMI (₹5,000) waived, then returns product on day 5
  - Refund = (Product price - paid EMIs) + ₹5,000 waiver credit
  - Refund = 100% of product price paid (including waived EMI)

### If Customer Wins EMI Waiver (After Return Initiated):
- **Draw eligibility:** Lost upon return/cancellation request
- **Previously waived EMIs:** Cannot be un-waived; customer keeps benefit

---

## 5. DAMAGE ASSESSMENT PROCESS

### Categories:
- **No damage:** Full refund (days 1-7)
- **Minor (scratches, dust):** 5-10% deduction
- **Moderate (dents, wood damage):** 15-30% deduction
- **Severe (structural damage):** 40-50% deduction
- **Irreparable:** Refuse refund (offer credit toward new purchase)

### Assessment:
- Conducted by trained staff (not salesperson)
- Documented with photos
- Customer present during assessment (if requested)
- Assessment report provided to customer within 2 days

---

## 6. REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINE

- **Return request approval:** Within 2 days
- **Inspection & assessment:** 2-3 days
- **Bank/gateway processing:** 2-7 business days
- **Total:** Maximum 14 days end-to-end

---

## 7. DAMAGED/DEFECTIVE ITEMS

If item arrives damaged/defective:
- **Immediate replacement:** No questions asked (CPA 2019)
- **Or refund:** Full refund within 7 days
- **Documentation:** Photo evidence + delivery receipt

---

## 8. ADVANCE PAYMENT & EARLY DELIVERY

### Advance Payment (Prepayment):
- Customer can pay extra to unlock early delivery
- Prepayment amount = stated by customer
- If customer returns within 7 days: Full prepayment refunded
- If customer returns after 7 days: Prepayment not refunded (applies to cancellation fee)

### Early Delivery Unlock:
- Upon prepayment, customer eligible for early delivery (within 3-5 days)
- If cancellation within 7 days: Full refund (per CPA 2019)
- If cancellation after 7 days: Prepayment applied to restocking fee

---

## 9. DISPUTES & ESCALATION

1. **Claim filed:** Within 7 days of return request
2. **Verification:** Finance team reviews (2-3 days)
3. **Assessment:** Damage inspection if needed
4. **Decision:** Communicated within 7 days
5. **Escalation:** Management review within 14 days
6. **Consumer court:** If unresolved (CPA 2019 jurisdiction)

---

## 10. SPECIAL CONDITIONS

### Non-Cancellable After Delivery Completion:
- If product delivered AND installation completed
- Customer has 7 days to return (CPA 2019)
- After 7 days: Cancellation non-viable (only damage claims)

### Lucky Draw & Cancellation:
- Once draw is committed (monthly), subscription continues
- Customer cannot cancel to avoid EMI if draw approaching
- But can return product within 7 days (CPA 2019)

### Made-to-Order Items:
- Customized furniture: Non-refundable after payment received
- Must be agreed in writing at subscription start
- Standard catalog items: Full CPA 2019 protection applies
